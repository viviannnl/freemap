RentMap — Craigslist rentals in Vancouver, on a map, by walking distance

RentMap is a free, interactive map of Craigslist's housing section for Vancouver, British Columbia. Drop a pin anywhere — your office, your campus, a SkyTrain station — and every rental is measured from that point, so you can see which apartments are genuinely within a ten-minute walk rather than merely in the same neighbourhood. Each pin is labelled with its rent, and each listing shows bedrooms, floor area where given, walking time, distance in metres, the neighbourhood, a photo and a link to the original Craigslist post.

How RentMap works

Filter by walking time, price range and bedroom count, including studios. Room shares, roommate posts and sublets are hidden by default. Listings spotted in the last half hour are marked NEW, pins are colour-coded by how recently they appeared, and a place that is reposted repeatedly is flagged as relisted, which often means it has not rented. You can also look up how old a building is, from the posting itself or from City of Vancouver property records.

RentMap only reads public listings and links out to Craigslist. Replying happens on Craigslist, which guards reply addresses behind a captcha.

What RentMap does not show

Craigslist's search endpoint returns at most 360 postings per request, and Vancouver usually has several thousand rentals listed at once. RentMap shows the most recent 360 and displays both numbers, so it is best for catching what has just been posted rather than for browsing the whole market.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find apartments within walking distance of my work?

Drag the pin onto your workplace; the walk slider then hides anything further away than the walking time you choose, allowing for street detours.

How can I find a studio or one-bedroom in downtown Vancouver?

Select the Studio and 1 bed chips and set your price range.

Looking for free stuff instead?

The same map covers Craigslist's free section: free stuff in Vancouver on a map — free furniture, electronics, building materials and moving boxes, live.
