FreeMap — a live map of free stuff on Craigslist in Vancouver

FreeMap is a free, interactive map of the Craigslist free section for Vancouver, British Columbia. Free items — furniture, electronics, building materials, moving boxes, bikes, plants and more — appear on the map in real time as they are posted. Each pin shows a photo, the neighbourhood, how long ago it was posted, and a link to the original Craigslist post.

How FreeMap works

Pins are colour-coded by freshness, from just-dropped to getting stale, and fade away when a listing disappears. Filter by category (furniture, electronics, materials, boxes, bikes) or by distance, and save finds to your stash. FreeMap only reads public listings and links out to Craigslist; replies happen on Craigslist.

Frequently asked questions

How can I find free stuff in Vancouver?

Open FreeMap to see every free Craigslist listing in Vancouver plotted on a live map, with the newest and nearest highlighted.

Where can I get free furniture near me in Vancouver?

Filter FreeMap by Furniture to map free sofas, chairs, dressers, tables and beds posted to Craigslist across Vancouver.

How do I find free moving boxes, firewood or building materials?

Filter by Boxes or Materials to show only those free listings on the map.
